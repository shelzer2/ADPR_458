Duolingo Campaign

This campaign repositions Duolingo as an experience you don’t just use; you keep coming back to it. By tapping into the idea of habit and repetition, the work highlights how quickly one lesson turns into many.

Problem / Challenge

Most people want to learn a new language but quit quickly. Language learning feels intimidating, time-consuming, and easy to abandon. Duolingo must motivate users to stick with it by reframing learning as fun, low-pressure, and part of everyday life.

Insight / Big Idea

When something feels good, you don’t want to stop.

Execution

The campaign reframes language learning through the lens of habit and repetition, using playful, chaotic visuals to highlight how engaging Duolingo becomes.

Young Ones Case Study

This campaign repositions Clash of Clans as a cultural force by extending the brand beyond the game and into real-world moments. By tapping into humor, chaos, and community, the work supports the Netflix series launch while making Clash feel relevant, inclusive, and impossible to ignore.

Problem

Clash of Clans needed to re-enter culture by making an inroads with females to make the Netflix series launch feel like a global entertainment moment, not just a game release.

Insight

Women want to feel belonging and compete within the gaming community, but they don't feel welcome.

Execution

Blending mainstream female culture with Clash of Clans brand activations and collaborations to show women they belong on the battlefield.

Barefoot Case Study

This campaign repositions Barefoot Wine as a bold, unexpected choice for St. Patrick’s Day by challenging traditional drinking norms and encouraging people to celebrate on their own terms.

Problem

Barefoot Wine is an overlooked and unconventional choice for St. Patrick's day.

Insight

Consumers don’t want to feel pressure to conform to traditional standards.

Execution

Give consumers a space and a reason to enjoy Barefoot wine without feeling out of place.

Plains FM

This campaign introduces Plains FM as a homespun radio station that celebrates slower living, using calm, immersive moments to create a sense of connection, space, and simplicity.

Problem

Many young Midwesterners feel culturally overlooked by national media and disconnected from platforms that reflect their pace of life and creative identity.

Insight

Slowing down isn’t a weakness; it’s a lifestyle people are craving but rarely see reflected in the hustle and bustle of the 21st century.

Execution

The campaign creates calm, immersive moments across everyday spaces, positioning Plains FM as a place to slow down and reconnect.
