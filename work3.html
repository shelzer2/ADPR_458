Copy and Concept

Barefoot Wine

A consumer-oriented pitch to drive Barefoot Wine consumption

Role Creative Strategy / Design
Timeline Spring 2026
Deliverables Print Ads / OOH Ads / Brand Activations
The Goal What was expected?

A strong and effective multichannel brand campaign that positions Barefoot Wine as a no-brainer beverage for the St. Patrick's Day holiday.

The Audience Who was it for?

This campaign targeted Gen-Z drinkers that traditionally consume beer to celebrate St. Patrick's Day.

The Outcome What was the result?

A consumer-oriented campaign that drove consumer desicions towards Barefoot Wine while creating an inroad within the beer-drinking community

Overview

Copy and Concept focuses on creative copywriting and execution while utilizing research and consumer perceptions to generate effective advertising campaigns.
